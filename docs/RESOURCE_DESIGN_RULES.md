# Words Have Weather printable resource rules

These rules apply to all 120 educator worksheets and all 120 parent practice workbooks in the complete learning journey.

## Shared concept

Each printable resource follows the same story movement:

1. recognise the pressured moment;
2. describe observable words or actions;
3. treat the child's possible experience as a possibility, not a fact;
4. retain the boundary, responsibility, consequence or safety instruction;
5. prepare one useful next sentence;
6. include a repair and escalation route where the short is not enough.

The resources teach adult reflection. They do not assess children, prescribe one universal script, replace professional care or imply government endorsement.

## Layout safety rules

- A4 portrait with a minimum 15 mm outer margin and a 12 mm protected footer zone.
- Body text is at least 8 pt; prompts are at least 8.2 pt; write-in labels are at least 8.3 pt.
- Every text component has a declared width, height, maximum line count and minimum font size.
- Text is measured before drawing. It may scale down only to its declared minimum; content that still does not fit stops generation with an error.
- Text never shares an unbounded horizontal row with an illustration.
- Page 4 transfer cards keep illustration, scene label, heading and description in separate bounded regions with at least 12 pt internal spacing.
- Write-in lines begin below the measured prompt, never at a fixed position that can collide with wrapped text.
- Headings are limited to two lines. Long wording is shortened editorially rather than squeezed below the minimum size.
- Colour supports hierarchy but never carries the only meaning.
- Raster illustrations are pre-sized as JPEG and embedded without decoding their compact DCT streams; optimisation must never rasterise or alter the vector text layer.
- File names use three-digit episode numbers (`episode-001` through `episode-120`) so website, metadata and bulk downloads sort consistently.

## Educator worksheet pattern - 5 pages

1. Cover and intended use.
2. Watch and notice: pressure line, possible weather, observable moment and retained need.
3. Build the next sentence: four episode-specific practice moves.
4. Transfer to practice: three low-stakes examples, staff discussion and adaptation.
5. Review before use: facilitation, safeguarding, alignment and release status.

## Parent practice workbook pattern - 6 pages

1. Cover and intended use.
2. Notice the adult pressure without making the child responsible for it.
3. Separate the event from identity and name the practical need.
4. Notice possible weather without mind-reading or diagnosing.
5. Keep the boundary and practise the episode-specific next sentence.
6. Repair, repeat and identify when context-specific support is needed.

## Release gate

Every episode must pass:

- page-by-page PDF rendering and visual inspection;
- automated overflow, page-count and text-presence checks;
- educator and parent usability review;
- safeguarding, accessibility and claims review;
- confirmation that alignment language says "aligned to" or "possible connection", never "approved" or "endorsed".
- confirmation that the episode status is enabled and its scheduled release date has arrived before a member download is served.
